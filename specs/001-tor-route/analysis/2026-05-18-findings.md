**I4 — Reference workstation: ≥8 cores vs 16 cores in SC-005 math.** **STILL OPEN.**
plan.md L70 baseline: "≥8 physical cores at ≥3 GHz". L85 SC-005 math plugs in "16 cores and N=1, that floor is ~2.5s wall-clock". 8 vs 16 isn't reconciled; could be "8 physical + SMT = 16 logical" but that isn't stated. At the 8-physical-core minimum, the N=4 fill-time math gives roughly 20s (still under the 30s pass criterion but tighter than implied).

*Fix*: Either change "16 cores" on L85 to "8 cores" and recompute, or change the reference baseline to "≥16 physical cores" on L70. The former is the safer documentation of the floor; the latter may exclude common dev hardware.

**I6 — Quickstart teardown lists 6 of 7 named volumes.** **STILL OPEN.**

**C1 — Multi-method SOCKS5 invariant under-tested.** **NEW.**
contracts/socks5-auth.md L132 says the invariant test asserts `0x05 0x02` "for clients offering `0x02`". The prose at L66–80 makes the multi-method case explicit ("offers `0x02` *anywhere in the methods list*"). But T023 exercises only the single-method case. A regression where the implementation accidentally accepts NO_AUTH when offered alongside USERNAME/PASSWORD would not be caught.

*Fix*: Either add a third row to the invariant table ("Methods response is exactly `0x05 0x02` for clients offering `[0x00, 0x02]` — USERNAME/PASSWORD selected even when NO_AUTH is also offered"), or rewrite T023's case list to include the mixed-method case explicitly.

**C2 — Outbound test-time HTTPS not documented as exempt from Principle III.** **NEW.**
T034f fetches the live Tor network consensus over HTTPS from `collector.torproject.org` — an outbound clearnet HTTP call from a test runner. Principle III bullet 6 ("Stargate makes no outbound network connections except those a request explicitly routes through") governs the *running system*, not test code, but the distinction is implicit; no spec/plan artefact records that outbound HTTPS from CI/test runners is permitted.

*Fix*: Add a one-sentence note to plan.md §"Constraints" L91–98 (the "No outbound network from Stargate components" bullet) clarifying that the constraint applies to running Stargate components, not test-time outbound fetches needed to construct gold-standard inputs (consensus, leak-corpus entries). Or move T034f's rationale paragraph into a `SECURITY-DECISIONS.md` entry.
